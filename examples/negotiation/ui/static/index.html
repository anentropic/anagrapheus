What am I looking at?

Two AI negotiators, one for the EU and one for the UK, bargain over a single clause. Each has a private goal, says things in public, and builds a picture of what the other side wants, and of what the other side thinks it knows.

Everything that happens is written to a log, and each side's beliefs are worked out from what it could have seen. So a side can be wrong, for example if it thinks a slip went unnoticed when it didn't. That gap is the point of the demo.

Step through with ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys. After the live run, the whole thing is replayed from the log alone: the same story, with no AI called at all.
